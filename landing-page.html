Refashion BXL

Four stops in the life of a garment, mapped across Brussels: find a second-hand shop to give a piece its second wardrobe, drop off the clothes you no longer wear so they can start a new journey, discover upcycling workshops that give an old pair of jeans, shirt or jacket a whole new story, or finally repair that bag, zipper or favourite piece you promised yourself you'd fix months ago. Browse by category or explore by neighbourhood and keep your clothes in the loop.
